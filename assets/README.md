# Images

- `glove-concept.webp`: Peter Neyra’s AI-generated product concept, accepted for Artisan Glove. The compact appearance concept is distinct from the dimensioned V4 engineering enclosure. MIT.
- `bela-pilot-signals.png`: Peter Neyra’s earlier physical Bela piezo experiment: nine saved 20-second takes, background/P60/P220, channel 2 at 44.1 kHz. The figure shows traces, normalized spectral density and RMS. Force and motion were unmeasured. Saved-clip classification and sustained live performance are separate results; the live grit classifier disagreed with its saved-clip evaluation. This is earlier experimental evidence, separate from V4 assembly or qualification. MIT.
