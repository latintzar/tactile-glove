# Dense tactile skin

The current investigation is a flexible, textured black nitrile precision glove targeting thousands of taxels across the hand. It retains the compact graphite wrist cuff, hand-facing camera and forest-green pull tab.

The jobs are recording surface texture and contact during hand work, studying delicate palpation in surgical training, and observing grip during sport. These application images illustrate the intended glove experience.

## Sensing layer and readout

The sensing layer, channel addressing, mechanical integration, routing and data acquisition remain current engineering questions. The dense portfolio view places 4,096 proposed taxel positions over one photograph. Those positions describe a layout target; acquired measurements belong to the experiment records.
