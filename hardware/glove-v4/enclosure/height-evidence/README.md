# Package-height evidence

All 545 former generic component instances are covered by 94 selected-MPN manufacturer maxima. The initial snapshot remains in package-heights.json; merged-package-heights.json/CSV are the current CAD inputs.

Every reduced envelope adds a separate 0.20 mm assembly allowance. This is an engineering assembly acceptance limit, not a manufacturer dimensional claim. The 15 original specific envelopes remain separately modeled; the new fuse and battery header are added by the connector revision.

Evidence includes cached manufacturer PDFs, distributor-hosted manufacturer PDFs, hashed catalog extracts and clearly labeled transcriptions of manufacturer drawings where direct downloads returned 403. Exact references, source URLs, physical pages and evidence hashes are retained in the merged table.
