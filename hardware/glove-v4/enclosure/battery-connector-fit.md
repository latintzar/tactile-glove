## Direct variant

Use the pack's existing AWG22 leads, two43030-0007 contacts and43025-0200 housing. Accept this variant only when each measured usable lead length **after crimp consumption** exceeds its routed centerline length by at least3mm, with the R4 bends, connector latch and strain relief accessible. The stock70±5mm specification alone is insufficient to assert that every supplied lead passes.

## Factory extension variant

If either direct lead fails reach, the assembler adds35mm of AWG22 wire to that lead using a5mm qualified overlap joint: **30mm effective reach gain per lead**. The stock65mm minimum then provides95mm before connector termination. For the fallback reach bound, reserve the entire14.25mm housing depth as an upper bound on lead consumed within the connector, leaving80.75mm external before the3mm strain-relief allowance. Use the same Micro-Fit housing/contacts. This is a separate factory assembly variant, not a second installed harness.

Stagger the two insulated joints in the left under-board bay: reserved sleeve envelopesØ4×16mm centered at(6.5,63,14) and(10.5,67,17). Use a qualified5mm soldered overlap joint with aTE Raychem ATUM-3/1-0-STK adhesive-lined sleeve cut16mm (accept recovered length14–16mm); both stripped conductors remain completely inside the sleeve. Maximum finished sleeveOD4mm. Immobilize each stiff section to the insulated carrier/battery padding with an adhesive textile strain-relief tab; start R4 bends beyond sleeve ends. The exactR4 routes pass through the full16mm straight sleeve sections; the exported control points and independently checked analytic lengths are in harness-envelopes.json. Dress and fixture those routes before terminating. Trim and join only in the assembler's protected-battery procedure.

The assembler must accept the splice material/process, insulation, retention and≤2mΩ resistance per joint, with pull/thermal checks in its qualified process. Root procurement includes 70mm additionalAWG22 and two joints in the source-path resistance budget. The direct path family is54.668–61.509mm.
