# Camera selection

The V4 design selects CamThink 73.002.000035: OV5640, 120° diagonal field of view and 8 cm preset focus. Verify the supplied tail outline, contacts and optics against the [camera interface](../interfaces/camera-interface.json) and the [manufacturer component guide](https://wiki.camthink.ai/docs/neoeyes-ne101-series/ne100-mb01-development-board/hardware-guide/components-overview/).

The public package preserves the component/interface and mechanical acceptance requirements. Obtain your own supplier quote and confirm the exact variant, tail, focus and stock before ordering.
